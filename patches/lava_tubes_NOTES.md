# lava_tubes.csv notes

10 lava tubes used as Moon/Mars analogs. Each source_link documents the analog use. Coordinates come from these sources:

| Site | Coordinates from |
|---|---|
| Cueva de los Verdes | Wikipedia (en) infobox |
| Cueva del Viento | showcaves.com |
| Lava Beds NM | Wikipedia infobox (monument, not a single cave entrance) |
| Lofthellir | grottomap.org entrance record |
| Surtshellir-Stefanshellir | showcaves.com (signs on Hallmundarhraun) |
| Craters of the Moon | showcaves.com (visitor centre; the caves are a few km south) |
| Mauna Loa tube | HI-SEAS habitat, Wikipedia. The NASA article doesn't give the tube's exact position, only "northern flank of Mauna Loa" |
| Pisgah | Wikipedia, Pisgah Crater (the mountain holds the lava tubes) |
| Shishan caves | showcaves.com, Xianren (Immortal) Cave. The other two caves are in the same volcanic group |
| Jingpo Lake | Centre of the study box 43°52'-44°20'N, 128°30'-129°00'E given in the paper |

Accuracy is site level (a few km). These are not GPS cave entrances, except Lofthellir and Cueva del Viento.
Sites considered and dropped because no analog-use link could be confirmed: Kazumura (Hawaii), Undara (Australia), Manjanggul (Jeju, which the Jingpo paper calls too modified by tourism).
