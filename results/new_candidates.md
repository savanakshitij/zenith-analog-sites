## Notes

- Percentile is the engine's area-weighted percentile of the cell's score among that target's eligible cells (the same figure as `percentile` in results/scores_balanced.json).
- score is the engine value at 4 decimals; score_display is the 2 decimal figure the app shows on cards.
- best_dimensions and worst_dimensions are the 2 highest and 2 lowest of the 7 dimension scores for that cell and target. Ties keep the engine's dimension order (thermal, aridity, terrain, surface, ice, illumination, geology).
- Overall list: highest score first, one entry per site; a site within 300 km of a higher-scoring one is listed in its `also_matches`.
- Names follow the app: crater name if the cell has a mapped impact crater, else the app's region table, else the country.
