Analog Lab

This is Zenith's idea in game form: find the place on Earth that best matches Mars.

How to play

The objective: a real mission will drill for evidence of ancient life on Mars, in Jezero crater. Before it flies, the drill is tested on Earth. Compare the numbers and choose the place closest to Mars. Its conditions are similar, so it's easy to drill. The wrong place is harder to dig.

  1. Compare the two real places' numbers (rain, cold, slope, rock, sunlight) with Mars.
  2. Pick the place closest to Mars's conditions, just like a real mission team would. Tap it or press 1 or 2.
  3. Drill. Tap DRILL or press Space as fast as you can for 10 seconds. Reach 20 m to finish.
  4. See your rank. The ranking board opens by itself.

Tip: looks can fool you. Trust the numbers.

All numbers are real, from our engine (data v1.6).
